This site is an independent study guide based on Matt Pocock’s workshop **Full Walkthrough: Workflow for AI Coding**.

## Primary source

- [Workshop video on YouTube](https://youtu.be/-QFHIoCo-Ko?t=178)
- The linked start point is 2:58, where the substantive workshop material begins.

## Related projects shown or discussed

- [Matt Pocock’s skills repository](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills)
- [Sandcastle agent orchestration project](https://github.com/mattpocock/sandcastle)

## Editorial approach

- The guide extracts fundamentals rather than reproducing the full workshop sequence.
- Topics are paraphrased, grouped, and normalized into a reusable engineering model.
- Practical checklists and decision rules are editorial additions derived from the extracted principles.
- The approximate 100K-token “smart zone” is a workshop heuristic, not a guaranteed universal threshold.
